Questions investors ask

Straight answers before you trust it with your watchlist.

Is this investment advice?
No. Auspex never tells you to buy or sell and never rates anything. It returns your own written reason, dated, against the facts, and ends with a question only you can answer.
How is it different from a dashboard or a screener?
A dashboard has opinions and shows state. Auspex has no opinion — it remembers yours, turns them into watchable lines, and checks them.
Do you score or rate stocks?
No score, no rating, no verdict is shown. The only judgement that matters here is yours.
What can it actually watch?
Honest answer: price and valuation lines have daily history, so they can be replayed and watched. Some fundamentals (growth, margins) update slowly and are watched going forward. Qualitative reasons (moat, management) stay yours — labelled manual, never faked as monitored.
Can I cancel anytime?
